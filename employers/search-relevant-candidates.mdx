---
title: "How do I search relevant candidates on Cutshort?"
description: "Start a talent search from a job description, set key requirements, add filters, and optionally run Top 50."
---

Go to **Talent database** → **Start a new search**, describe the role, set key requirements, then use filters or **Top 50** to get a shortlist you can act on.

## 1. Start a new search

Open [cutshort.io/profile/candidate-search/new](https://cutshort.io/profile/candidate-search/new), or use **Talent database** → **Start a new search**.

![Describe the role you are hiring for, with optional experience, location, salary, and skills](/images/search-relevant-1-start.png)

Paste a job description or write a short role brief in the box. You can also set optional hard filters here:

- **Experience range**
- **Locations** — **For job locations** or **Anywhere**
- **Annual salary range**
- **Skills** (up to 2)

Click **Search candidates**. The button stays disabled until the description is at least 50 characters.

## 2. Enter key requirements

Cutshort generates a first set of requirements from what you wrote. Edit them before the search starts.

![Key requirements modal with mandatory and preferred criteria, plus Add a criterion](/images/search-relevant-2-key-requirements.png)

Write anything you would expect to see on a resume, for example:

- Skills (Node.js, Figma, SQL)
- Kind of work done (built APIs, owned a growth funnel, shipped a mobile app)
- Team leading or management experience
- College tier
- Industry or domain (fintech, SaaS, healthcare)

Each line is auto-classified as **Mandatory** or **Preferred**. Use **+ Add a criterion** for anything missing. Click **Save and start search** when you are ready.

You can change these later from a talent card with **Edit key requirements**.

## 3. Tighten results with filters

The results page shows relevant candidates for this search. Use the left **Filters** panel to narrow the list.

![Candidate search results with Skills, Experience, Location, Salary, Notice period, and Boolean search filters](/images/search-relevant-3-filters.png)

Common filters include:

- **Boolean search** — exact words from the resume (for example `(kafka OR "event-driven") AND node`)
- **Skills**
- **Experience**
- **Salary**
- **Location**
- **Notice period**
- **Industry**
- **Education pedigree**
- **Candidate activity**

**Remove all filters** clears the panel. For industry, college tier, job switching, startup background, and the other special filters, see [How do I use search filters to find more relevant candidates?](/employers/search-filters).

Open a [talent card](/employers/what-is-a-talent-card) to read the resume before you unlock or invite.

## 4. Use Top 50 to shortlist

Flip the toggle from **All relevant** to **Top 50**. Cutshort reviews the search results against your key requirements and keeps people who match the mandatory ones.

![Top 50 on, with Export to google sheet and Add all to a job pipeline](/images/search-relevant-4-top-50.png)

When it finishes you will see **Successfully found top 50 after reviewing [N] most relevant candidates.** Then you can:

- **Export to google sheet**
- **Add all to a job pipeline**

For the full Top 50 flow, limits, and what to do if no one matches, see [How do I find Top 50 candidates who match all my requirements?](/employers/find-top-50-candidates).

## If something goes wrong

- **Search candidates is disabled** — add more detail to the role description (50+ characters).
- **Could not generate requirements** — add them with **+ Add a criterion**, or start the search without them.
- **List is too broad** — add a boolean, skill, location, salary, or notice-period filter, or turn on **Top 50**.
- **List is too thin** — remove a filter, or click **Edit criteria** / **Edit key requirements** and relax a mandatory line.

Something else? Chat with us on [cutshort.io](https://cutshort.io).
