If you want to **hire** through Cutshort — or want someone from our team to **call you** about plans, pricing, or how hiring works — this page is for you.

## How employer hiring plans work

Cutshort offers two ways to hire:

### Freedom Plan (self-serve)

For teams that want to post jobs, search candidates, and run hiring themselves on Cutshort.

- Unlimited job posts and candidate unlocks for your company (no per-seat or per-resume meter on this plan)
- One flat price based on company size
- Your whole hiring team can use the platform

See overview and current pricing: [cutshort.io/pricing](https://cutshort.io/pricing)

### Assisted hiring (we help you hire)

For teams that want Cutshort’s team to source and shortlist candidates for a role.

- We handle sourcing, shortlisting, and coordination
- Fee is tied to a successful hire (details on the pricing page)

See [cutshort.io/pricing](https://cutshort.io/pricing) for how Assisted hiring is described today.

## Request a callback (pricing, demo, or “call me about jobs”)

If you want to **talk to someone** — pricing, which plan fits, demo, or how to get started — request a callback here:

**[cutshort.io/a/generic-emp-form](https://cutshort.io/a/generic-emp-form)**

Share your **work email** and **phone number** on that form. Our team will reach out.

We do not schedule meetings through the help chat. Use the callback form above.

## Already a Cutshort customer?

If you already have an employer account and need help with billing, plan activation, or using the product, email [support@cutshort.io](mailto:support@cutshort.io) from your work email.

For payment-link activation issues, see [I purchased the Starter plan using a link](/employers/plan-activation).

## Looking for a job (not hiring)?

If you are a **job seeker**, employer pricing does not apply to you. See [Is Cutshort paid for job seekers?](/candidates/is-cutshort-paid-for-job-seekers).
