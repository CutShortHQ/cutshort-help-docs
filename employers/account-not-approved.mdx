Since companies get access to the candidate pool, we need to ensure that only real people from genuine companies get access to the platform.

To do this, some checks we do are:

1. You should register from a work email. Public email services like gmail, yahoo etc, your access request will be rejected.
2. The company website you submit should be same as the domain in your email. If your email is abc@[xyz.com](http://xyz.com) but you have given website name as amazon.com, your access request will be rejected.
3. The company website you submitted should load and should have credibility along with names/social media links of key team members.

If your request was declined, please check the above reasons and create another account to fix the issues, if any.

Chat with us, if your is a special case that we should consider.
