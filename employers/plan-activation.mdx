---
title: "I purchased the Starter plan using a link. How will it get activated?"
description: "Activation is automatic - just provide your Cutshort account email"
---

While making the payment using the payment link, you will be asked for your Cutshort registered email address. If you provide this correctly, As soon as you make the payment, the system will map your account and will activate your new plan on it. 

But if you provide a different email address that does not exist on CutShort, this activation will have to be done manually. Please email [cs@cutshort.io](mailto:cs@cutshort.io) if your account is not activated automatically within 10 mins.
