The Free plan lets you keep using Cutshort and check the talent pool whenever you have roles to close. It is not meant for running engineering hiring at full strength.

## Why Free exists

Free is ongoing access — not a trial that expires. You can stay on it, post roles when you need to, and look at the talent pool without paying a subscription.

What it is not: a full hiring workspace. Searching the talent database, unlocking profiles, and messaging candidates are tightly capped.

## What you can do on Free

You can **post jobs**. You cannot use the talent database much.

Candidates who **apply to your job** are often free to unlock — especially for **non-tech** roles, or when the candidate’s current salary is under **₹10L**. For those applicants you can usually see contact details without spending your monthly unlocks.

For **engineering** roles, Free is not enough. You will hit unlock, message, and search limits quickly. Upgrading to Freedom Plan is the recommended path if you are hiring engineers.

## Free plan limits (per monthly cycle)

These are the product limits on Free today:

| What | Free limit |
|---|---|
| Job postings | You can post jobs |
| Talent-database unlocks | **3** contact/resume unlocks |
| Messages / invites to talent-database candidates | **0** — you cannot start outreach from search |
| Talent-database search | **2 pages** of results, then search is blocked |
| Recruiter logins | **5** |
| AI reviews while searching | **50** profile reviews |
| Applicants to your jobs | Usually unlockable without using the 3 credits (especially non-tech / under ₹10L) |

Unlocks and messages reset when your plan cycle renews (about once a month). Already-unlocked people stay unlocked.

API and MCP (if your account is approved) use the **same unlock and message pool**. `GET /usage` shows what you can still spend.

## Freedom Plan

Freedom Plan removes the meter. One flat price based on your **company size** — starting at **₹10,000/month**.

Always included:

- Unlimited job postings
- Unlimited candidate unlocks (fair usage)
- No seat limits — the whole hiring team can use Cutshort
- No salary caps on who you can unlock

See current prices by company size: [cutshort.io/pricing](https://cutshort.io/pricing)

If you are already signed in, open **Billing → Subscription plans** to upgrade: [cutshort.io/profile/employer-plans](https://cutshort.io/profile/employer-plans)

More on the model: [Introducing Freedom Plan](https://cutshort.io/blog/startup-hiring/introducing-freedom-plan-pay-for-value-not-for-accessing-millions-of-resumes)

Cutshort also offers **Assisted** hiring (our team sources and shortlists; **8.33% of offered CTC**, no retainer). Details are on the [pricing page](https://cutshort.io/pricing).
