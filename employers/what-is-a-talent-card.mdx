---
title: "What is a Talent Card?"
description: "Think of the Talent Card as a candidate’s professional snapshot."
---

Think of the Talent Card as a candidate’s professional snapshot. It helps you get a quick, yet comprehensive, view of a candidate without having to dig through long resumes right away. Here's an example:

![what is a talent card screenshot 1](/images/what-is-a-talent-card-1.png)

Here's what you’ll find at a glance:

- ✅ Total experience 

- 🎓 Education 

- 🧳 Work history 

- 💰 Current and expected compensation

- 📍 Preferred location or remote preference

- 🟢 Availability status 

- 📄 Resume (with masked contact details)

**Why it matters:** The Talent Card gives you a focused view to decide whether a candidate is worth pursuing — helping you shortlist faster and with more confidence.

**Pro Tip:** While the Talent Card gives a comprehensive view, do check out the resume of the candidate while deciding their relevance. You can do so by clicking on "📄Resume" as shown in the screenshot below.

![what is a talent card screenshot 2](/images/what-is-a-talent-card-2.png)
