Unlike large platforms such as LinkedIn, which have generic filters, Cutshort has designed filters for tech industry recruiters and founders who look for solid and skilled talent.

After you start a search, the left **Filters** panel on the results page is where these options live. For the full search flow, see [How do I search relevant candidates on Cutshort?](/employers/search-relevant-candidates#3-tighten-results-with-filters).

Open **Talent database** → a saved search, or start a new one from [cutshort.io/profile/candidate-search/new](https://cutshort.io/profile/candidate-search/new).

## First, an important recommendation

First use filters like **Skills**, **Industry**, **Location**, and **Education pedigree** to narrow the talent pool.

For custom and specific criteria — such as “Experience in ML models, not just using LLMs” — use **Key requirements** for best accuracy.

## Skills

If you already know the concrete skills for the role, add them under **Skills**. Candidates with those skills rank higher.

Use **Boolean search** when you need the exact words on the resume — for example `(kafka OR "event-driven") AND node`. That catches mentions in job descriptions and projects, not only tagged skills.

Do not stack too many skill groups. Two to four is enough. Prefer `python` over `python 3.10`.

## Key requirements

Use **Key requirements** for custom and specific criteria a resume can show — not just a skill tag. Example: “Experience in ML models, not just using LLMs.” Other examples: owned a payments API, led a team of 5, worked in a GCC, or went to a tier-1 college.

Set these when you start the search, or later with **Edit key requirements** / **Edit criteria**.

Each line is **Mandatory** or **Preferred**. Mandatory lines hide people who do not match.

To surface people who match every mandatory line without reading cards one by one, flip **All relevant** to **Top 50**. Cutshort reviews the search results for you. See [How do I find Top 50 candidates who match all my requirements?](/employers/find-top-50-candidates).

Top 50 uses AI review credits. Freedom plans include 10,000 AI review credits per month. If you hit the limit, email [hire@cutshort.io](mailto:hire@cutshort.io).

## Industry

**Industry** is built for tech recruiters. It is not a generic “IT / non-IT” list. Pick the backgrounds you want, such as:

- **B2B SaaS**
- **Software product**
- **Consumer internet**
- **GCC**
- **E-commerce**
- **Banking and finance**
- **Healthcare**
- **Edtech and education**
- **Gaming**
- **Computer security**
- **Travel**

To hide IT-services backgrounds, use:

- **Exclude large IT services** — companies like Infosys, Cognizant, TCS
- **Exclude all IT services** — large and smaller IT-services / consulting firms

## Education pedigree

Do not type college names. A name list misses global schools such as Caltech or Berkeley.

Under **Education pedigree**, pick a tier:

- **Tier 1+ colleges** — topmost tech institutes, such as top IITs, IISc, BITS Pilani, MIT, Purdue, Caltech
- **Tier 1 colleges** — the next band, such as other IITs and recognised NITs
- **Tier 2 colleges** — other reputed tech institutes

You can select more than one tier.

## Job switching

**Job switching** hides people who have a pattern of short tenures. Turn on **Hide frequent job switchers**.

Use this when you want a more stable work history. Leave it off if short stints are fine for the role.

## Startup experience and entrepreneurial experience

These are two filters.

**Startup experience**

- **Funded startups** — worked at a funded or well-known startup
- **Other startups** — startup experience outside that set

**Entrepreneurial experience**

- **Cofounded a venture** — started a company
- **Early stage member** — played a core role in the early stage of a venture

Use one or both, depending on whether you want company-type (startup) or founder / early-employee experience.

## Work quality

**Work quality** looks at the strength of achievements on the profile.

- **Extremely high** — remarkable achievement on a professional task
- **High** — notable achievement

This is a preference filter. Start with the broader search, then add it if the list is still too wide.

## Career growth

**Career growth** keeps people who have shown relatively fast progression. The option is **Fast career growth**.

Use it for roles where pace of promotion or expanding scope matters.

## Gender

**Gender** filters by **Male**, **Female**, **Other**, or **Undisclosed / NA**.

Candidates who have not shared gender appear under **Undisclosed / NA**.

## If something goes wrong

- **List collapsed to almost no one** — remove one filter at a time. Industry exclude + pedigree + must-have skills stack quickly.
- **Industry looks wrong** — these tags are from the profile, not the current employer name alone. Try a nearby tag (for example **Software product** and **B2B SaaS**).
- **College you care about is missing** — pick the tier, do not type the school name.
- **Top 50 is out of credits** — Freedom includes 10,000 AI review credits per month. Email [hire@cutshort.io](mailto:hire@cutshort.io) if you need more.
