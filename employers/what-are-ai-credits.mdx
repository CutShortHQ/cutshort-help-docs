---
title: "What are AI credits on a Freedom plan?"
description: "Which optional hiring features use AI credits, and how to buy more when the included credits run out."
---

Some optional features, such as Top 50 candidate finder, use AI credits. If you run out of them, you can buy them separately.

Freedom plans include **10,000 AI credits each month**, for every company size.

## What uses AI credits

**Top 50** in talent search. Cutshort reviews your most relevant results and keeps only people who match every mandatory requirement. See [How do I find Top 50 candidates who match all my requirements?](/employers/find-top-50-candidates).

## If you run out

The screen says you are out of AI credits for this feature. Email [hire@cutshort.io](mailto:hire@cutshort.io) to buy more.

Something else? Chat with us on [cutshort.io](https://cutshort.io).
