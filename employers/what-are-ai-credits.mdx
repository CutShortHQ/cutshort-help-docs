---
title: "What are AI credits on a Freedom plan?"
description: "AI credits are used when Cutshort reviews talent database profiles against your key requirements. Each profile uses 1 credit, including Top 50."
---

AI credits are used when Cutshort reviews a candidate profile against your key requirements. That happens while you browse talent database search results. Each profile reviewed uses 1 AI credit.

**Top 50** uses the same credits. Turning it on starts reviewing those search results automatically, so Cutshort can find the top 50 who match every mandatory requirement. See [How do I find Top 50 candidates who match all my requirements?](/employers/find-top-50-candidates).

If you run out, you can buy more separately.

Freedom plans include AI credits each month based on company size.

| Company size | AI credits each month |
| --- | --- |
| 1–19 employees | 5,000 |
| 20–99 employees | 10,000 |
| 100–499 employees | 10,000 |
| 500+ employees | 10,000 |

## If you run out

The screen says you are out of AI credits for this feature. Email [hire@cutshort.io](mailto:hire@cutshort.io) to buy more.

Something else? Chat with us on [cutshort.io](https://cutshort.io).
