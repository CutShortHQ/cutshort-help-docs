AI Interviews are used by companies on Cutshort to validate your experience. It's not an assessment like HackerRank, Codility etc but a simple conversation in AI tries to understand your experience better.
**Why we recommend completing AI interviews**

When you are invited for an AI interview, we highly recommend taking it. 
AI interviews are designed for a fast experience. Most candidates complete it within 12-15 mins. You can do it from your phone too.
Investing these 15 mins helps you:

- Know the key requirements that the company has in that job position
- Stand out from other candidates and get a fast track experience from the hiring company

**Do not attempt to cheat**
We have seen 10,000+ AI interviews in last 3 months on Cutshort and know there are around 1% cases where candidates try to get creative. 

We silently capture many pieces of information about your behavior and responses. When we see foul play, your interview results are discarded and your profile is flagged. This may result in a drastically low success rate for you on Cutshort.
