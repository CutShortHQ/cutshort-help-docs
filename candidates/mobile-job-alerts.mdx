We have native mobile apps

We highly recommend installing our Android and iOS apps for fast job alerts and notifications from recruiters.

**IO/Android**
Android: [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cutshort](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cutshort)
iOS: [https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cutshort-ai-job-search-app/id6446403995](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cutshort-ai-job-search-app/id6446403995)

**WhatsApp notifications**
We also recommend turning on WhatsApp notifications
Go to your [Settings > Notifications](https://cutshort.io/profile/candidate-settings/notifications) page to do this.
