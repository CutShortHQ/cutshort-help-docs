---
title: "Does Cutshort offer paid job search, career coaching, or resume services?"
description: "What is free on Cutshort for job seekers, and which paid candidate services we do not offer today."
---

If you are **looking for a job**, this is what Cutshort offers — and what we **do not** offer as paid services today.

## What is free for job seekers

You can sign up, create your talent card, browse jobs, and apply **without paying** for job search on Cutshort.

To find roles, use **Find jobs → View jobs**:

[cutshort.io/profile/recommended-jobs](https://cutshort.io/profile/recommended-jobs)

See [How do I search and filter jobs on Cutshort?](/candidates/search-and-filter-jobs) for filters and tips.

Job alerts can come through the product, email, WhatsApp, or the mobile app — see [Getting fast job alerts on mobile](/candidates/mobile-job-alerts).

## Paid services we do not provide (candidates)

Cutshort **does not currently offer** paid services for job seekers such as:

- **Paid plans to find jobs for you** (or paid “we will find jobs and call you” services)
- **Career advice** or career coaching
- **Interview preparation** or coaching
- **Mock interviews**
- **Resume review**
- **Resume drafting** or resume writing

If you asked about any of these, we are not able to provide them on Cutshort today.

## Updating your own resume on Cutshort

You can **upload or update your resume** on your talent card yourself — see [How to update your resume](/candidates/update-resume). That is part of your profile, not a paid resume-writing service.

If upload fails, see [I'm not able to upload my resume](/candidates/resume-upload-issues).

## AI interviews from companies

Some **companies** on Cutshort may invite you to an **AI screener** as part of their hiring process. That is set up by the employer, not a paid Cutshort coaching product. See [What are AI interviews and how do they help me?](/candidates/what-are-ai-interviews).

## Need help using Cutshort?

For account or how-to questions, email [support@cutshort.io](mailto:support@cutshort.io) from your registered Cutshort email.

If you are **hiring for a company** and meant to ask about employer plans or a sales callback, see [I need a paid hiring service or want to speak to someone about pricing](/employers/hiring-plans-pricing-and-callback).
